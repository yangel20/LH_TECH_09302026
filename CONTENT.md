## Problem
Customers increasingly ask AI assistants what to buy instead of searching and clicking.
"Customers may never visit a company's website if the AI assistant does not mention that
company, or if it describes the product incorrectly." (challenge brief)

Two risks:
- **Lost visibility**: the product is never mentioned, so the customer never arrives.
- **Brand damage**: the AI hallucinates wrong pricing, features, availability or policies.

Traditional metrics (SEO traffic, clicks, conversions) no longer show this.

## Proposed solution
Nexo is a B2B Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform. It checks what AI assistants say
about a company's products, compares that against verified product information, finds the
source gaps that cause omissions or errors, and guides approved fixes, then re-tests to prove
they worked.

## How it works (4 steps, used in the Features section)
1. **Monitor**: Query major AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) with realistic customer
   questions. Track whether the client's products appear, which competitors are recommended,
   and what claims the AI makes.
2. **Analyze**: Compare AI answers against the client's verified product information. Flag
   discrepancies in features, pricing, availability and policies.
3. **Investigate**: Trace the root cause. Example: a product page lists battery capacity in
   watt-hours but never states battery life in hours.
4. **Optimize**: Recommend specific updates to product pages, retailer listings, FAQs and feeds.
   The client reviews and approves every change, then Nexo re-tests to confirm improvement.

## What makes us different
"Unlike other tools that simply report product omissions, Nexo goes further by checking AI
claims against verified product information, tracing problems to likely source gaps, and
guiding approved corrections," while measuring outcomes through repeated testing.

## Example (drives the demo page)
- Customer asks an AI assistant: "Best gaming laptop under $1,500 with at least 6 hours of battery life?"
- The client's laptop qualifies but is **not mentioned**.
- Nexo finds the cause: the product page lists battery as **watt-hours** with no estimated runtime,
  so the AI can't confirm "6+ hours".
- Recommendation: add verified battery life in hours to the product page and feeds.
- Re-test: the laptop now appears in the AI recommendation.

## Governance and ethics
- **Automated monitoring**: detects hallucinations and accuracy issues continuously
- **Human review**: gates all customer-facing content changes before deployment
- **Escalation protocols**: separate urgent issues from routine updates
- **Accountability assignment**: clear responsibility when errors occur
- **Trust metrics**: measure customer confidence and business outcomes alongside visibility
- **Scalability safeguards**: oversight stays effective as Nexo expands across products and AI systems

## Business impact metrics
- Inclusion rate in AI recommendations
- Conversion improvements from AI-referred traffic
- Reduced AI hallucination incidents
- Customer trust indicators
- Sustained accuracy over time

Nexo transforms how companies compete in AI-mediated commerce, where visibility and accuracy
determine customer acquisition and retention.
